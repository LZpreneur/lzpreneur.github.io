Second Month Meeting Collection

Entering the second month of entrepreneurship, intensively collecting data

20250311

Discussed:

  • Tasks to complete for the week’s Sprint
  • Review survey collection status
  • MVP feature brainstorming discussion
  • Discussion on development technology framework scope

This day’s meeting reviewed user survey content, brainstorming with wild creativity, discussing what users need most.

Looking at survey feedback, most mentioned lack of standardized formats, not knowing how to write, requirements easily misunderstood, etc.

This made us think about our product’s goal, which is:

Improve document readability!

Not only easy for authors to write, but also easy for readers to read - this is the direction we want to take our product.

We also thought about how there are many tools, and everyone excels at different ones. The most important thing is communication ability.

Whether it’s verbal expression, written communication, or even body language, all are part of communication.

What we want to do is:

Enable authors to write as smoothly as flowing water, while ensuring content readability gives readers a comfortable reading experience.
