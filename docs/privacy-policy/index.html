Privacy Policy

Welcome to "SAD-AI" (hereinafter referred to as this website). To ensure you can use the various services and information on this website with peace of mind, we hereby explain our privacy protection policy to safeguard your rights. Please read the following content carefully:

1. Scope of Privacy Protection Policy​

The privacy protection policy covers how this website handles personally identifiable information collected when you use our website services. This privacy protection policy does not apply to linked websites outside of this website, nor does it apply to personnel not commissioned by or participating in the management of this website.

2. Collection, Processing, and Use of Personal Data​

  • When you visit this website or use the functional services provided by this website, we will request necessary personal data based on the nature of the service function, and process and use your personal data within the scope of that specific purpose. Without your written consent, this website will not use personal data for other purposes.
  • When you use interactive functions such as service mailboxes and surveys, this website will retain the name, email address, contact information, and usage time you provide.
  • During general browsing, servers automatically record relevant activities, including the IP address of your connected device, usage time, browser used, browsing and click data records, etc., as reference for improving our website services. These records are for internal use only and will never be disclosed publicly.
  • To provide accurate services, we will conduct statistics and analysis on collected survey content. Statistical data or explanatory text from analysis results, in addition to internal research, may be published as needed for statistical data and explanatory text, but will not involve specific individual data.

3. Data Protection​

  • This website's servers are equipped with firewalls, antivirus systems, and other relevant information security equipment and necessary security protection measures to protect the website and your personal data with strict protection measures. Only authorized personnel can access your personal data, and all relevant processing personnel have signed confidentiality agreements. Those who violate confidentiality obligations will be subject to relevant legal penalties.
  • When it is necessary to commission other units to provide services due to business needs, this website will also strictly require them to comply with confidentiality obligations and take necessary inspection procedures to ensure they will indeed comply.

4. External Links from the Website​

This website's pages provide links to other websites, and you can also click through links provided by this website to enter other websites. However, those linked websites are not subject to this website's privacy protection policy, and you must refer to the privacy protection policy of the linked website.

5. Policy on Sharing Personal Data with Third Parties​

This website will never provide, exchange, rent, or sell any of your personal data to other individuals, groups, private enterprises, or government agencies, except where there is legal basis or contractual obligation.

The exceptions mentioned in the previous paragraph include but are not limited to:

  • With your written consent.
  • As explicitly required by law.
  • To eliminate danger to your life, body, freedom, or property.
  • Cooperation with government agencies or academic research institutions, based on public interest for statistical or academic research purposes when necessary, and the data has been processed by the provider or collector in a way that makes it impossible to identify specific parties according to their disclosure method.
  • When your behavior on the website violates terms of service or may damage or hinder the rights of the website and other users or cause harm to anyone, and website management determines that disclosing your personal data is necessary for identification, contact, or legal action.
  • Benefits your interests.
  • When this website commissions vendors to assist in collecting, processing, or using your personal data, we will exercise due supervision and management responsibility over outsourced vendors or individuals.

6. Use of Cookies​

To provide you with the best service, this website will place and access our cookies on your computer. If you do not wish to accept cookie writing, you can set the privacy level to high in your browser's function settings to refuse cookie writing, but this may cause some website functions to not operate normally.

7. Amendments to Privacy Protection Policy​

This website's privacy protection policy will be amended as needed from time to time, and amended terms will be published on the website.
