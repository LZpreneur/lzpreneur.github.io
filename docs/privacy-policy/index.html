Website Privacy Policy

Welcome to the official website of LZpreneur OÜ (hereinafter referred to as “this website”). This Privacy Policy explains how this website collects, uses and protects your personal data when you access or use the services and information provided here. Please read the following carefully before using this website.

1. Scope of this Privacy Policy​

This Privacy Policy applies to the collection, processing and use of personal data when you browse or use this website, including lzpreneur.io and its subdomains.

It does not apply to third‑party websites linked from this website, nor to individuals or organisations that are not managed, controlled or commissioned by this website. For those, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

2. Collection, processing and use of personal data​

2.1 Information you provide​

When you use interactive features provided by this website (such as contact forms, service mailboxes, surveys or newsletter subscriptions), we may ask you to provide certain personal data. Depending on the feature, this may include (but is not limited to):

  • Name or nickname
  • Email address
  • Contact details
  • The content of your message or inquiry
  • The time of submission and related usage records

We only use this information for specific purposes related to the services you request (for example, replying to your inquiry, providing support or sending you subscribed information).

We will not use your personal data for any other purposes that are unrelated to the original purpose without your prior explicit consent.

2.2 Automatically collected technical data​

When you browse this website, our hosting provider or CDN may generate basic connection logs (such as your IP address and access time). These logs are used solely for the following necessary purposes:

  • Maintaining network and information security (for example, detecting abnormal traffic or attack patterns)
  • Monitoring and improving the stability and performance of our services
  • Generating aggregate, non‑identifying statistics for service improvement

These records are stored only for a reasonable period of time and are then deleted or anonymised. They are not used for marketing purposes or to create individual user profiles.

3. Use of cookies and similar technologies​

To provide you with a better user experience, this website may set and read cookies or use similar technologies on your device, for purposes such as:

  • Remembering your settings and preferences (for example, language or display options)
  • Improving the functionality and performance of the website
  • Supporting advertising and measurement services (for example, Google AdSense), in compliance with applicable laws and platform requirements

You can manage cookies through your browser settings (for example, by blocking some or all cookies, or deleting existing cookies). Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the proper functioning of some features of this website.

4. Purposes and legal bases under GDPR (for EEA / UK users)​

If you are located in the European Union (EU), the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK), the processing of your personal data by this website will be based on the following legal grounds under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

4.1 Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR)​

  • Non‑essential cookies, website analytics and personalised advertising (for example, certain Google AdSense features) are only activated after you have given your explicit opt‑in consent via a cookie banner or consent management interface.
  • You may change or withdraw your consent at any time through the cookie/privacy settings made available on the website. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent prior to its withdrawal.

4.2 Contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)​

  • Processing the information that you submit via contact forms, service mailboxes or similar features, in order to respond to your inquiries, provide the services or information you request, and carry out related follow‑up communications.

4.3 Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)​

  • Maintaining the security and stable operation of this website (for example, detecting abnormal usage or attacks).
  • Analysing usage on an aggregated and anonymised basis to improve the content and user experience of the website.

In these cases, we will ensure that our legitimate interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.

5. Third‑party services and Google AdSense​

This website may use third‑party services and tools, including but not limited to the following:

5.1 Google AdSense​

  • Used to display advertisements on this website, measure ad performance, and help prevent invalid clicks and abnormal traffic.
  • May use cookies, online identifiers and device information for these purposes.
  • The collection and use of such data are governed by Google’s own privacy policy and terms.

5.2 Other analytics and tools (if enabled)​

  • If this website uses analytics services (for example, for traffic or performance analysis), they will only be activated where appropriate and, for EEA/UK users, generally only after obtaining your consent.
  • Where possible, data will be processed in an aggregated or pseudonymised form for statistical purposes.

Third‑party service providers may process or store your data on their own servers, and their processing is subject to their respective privacy policies and data protection agreements. Where cross‑border data transfers are involved, appropriate safeguards (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses) will be implemented as required by applicable law.

6. Sharing and disclosure of personal data​

We do not sell, rent, exchange or otherwise disclose your personal data to third parties, except in the following circumstances:

  1. With your prior explicit consent or authorisation.
  2. Where required by applicable laws, regulations or competent authorities.
  3. Where necessary to prevent or respond to serious threats to your life, body, liberty or property.
  4. Where necessary for public interest statistical or academic research, provided that the data has been processed so that no specific individual can be identified.
  5. Where your actions on this website violate applicable terms of use or may harm the rights, property or safety of this website, other users or any third party, and disclosure is reasonably necessary for identification, contact or legal action.
  6. In other situations where it is necessary to protect your vital interests and permitted by law.
  7. Where we engage third‑party vendors or partners to provide services or process personal data on our behalf; in such cases, we will supervise them in accordance with applicable laws and require them to comply with confidentiality and data security obligations, and to use the data only for the specific purposes of our engagement.

7. Data security measures​

To protect your personal data, this website implements appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including but not limited to:

  • Deploying firewalls, anti‑malware and intrusion detection or prevention tools
  • Using HTTPS/TLS encryption to protect data in transit
  • Restricting access rights so that only authorised personnel with a need to know can access personal data
  • Requiring relevant personnel to sign confidentiality undertakings, with legal and contractual consequences for breaches

When we need to entrust third parties to process personal data, we also require them to adopt security and confidentiality measures that are at least equivalent to our own standards.

8. Data retention​

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected, to perform contracts, or to comply with legal retention obligations. After the relevant period expires or the purpose no longer exists, we will delete or anonymise the data in accordance with applicable laws and our internal procedures. In general:

  • Contact / inquiry / subscription data: Retained for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide follow‑up services or comply with legal or regulatory requirements, or until you withdraw your consent or request deletion (where applicable).
  • Cookies and related identifiers: Retained for the duration specified for each cookie type and then automatically deleted or transformed so that they can no longer identify an individual.
  • Hosting or CDN connection logs: Retained only for a reasonable period necessary for security monitoring and troubleshooting, and then deleted or anonymised.

9. Your rights under GDPR (EEA / UK users)​

If you are located in the EU/EEA or the UK and the GDPR applies to you, you may, within the limits of applicable law, have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: To obtain confirmation as to whether we process your personal data and, if so, access to that data and related information.
  • Right to rectification: To request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”): To request deletion of your personal data where certain legal conditions are met.
  • Right to restriction of processing: To request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in specific situations.
  • Right to data portability: Where legally applicable, to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine‑readable format and to transmit that data to another controller.
  • Right to object: To object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise any of the above rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 11. We will respond within a reasonable period (in principle, not exceeding 30 days), in accordance with applicable laws.

10. International data transfers​

Your personal data may be processed or stored on servers located outside your country or region (for example, where third‑party service providers’ data centres are located outside the EU/EEA).

Where cross‑border transfers occur, we will implement appropriate safeguards as required by applicable data protection laws, such as the use of EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or other legally recognised transfer mechanisms, to provide an adequate level of protection for your data.

11. Children’s privacy​

This website is not directed at children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided personal data to this website without your consent, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete such data in accordance with applicable laws.

12. Contact us​

If you have any questions, comments or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our handling of personal data, or if you wish to exercise any rights under GDPR or other applicable laws, please contact us at:

  • Email: support@lzpreneur.io

If you are located in the EU/EEA or the UK and believe that our processing of your personal data infringes GDPR, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If we are legally required to appoint an EU representative in the future, we will update this section with the representative’s contact details.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy​

We may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time in response to service changes, legal or regulatory updates, or other operational needs.

The latest version will always be published on this page and will indicate the date of the most recent update. Your continued use of this website after any changes take effect will be deemed as your acknowledgement and acceptance of the revised Policy.
